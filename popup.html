Forces voice dictation to listen for this language, regardless of your browser's setting. Recognition quality depends on your browser's speech engine — Chrome supports these well, but rarer languages may not be available on all systems.
How to use: Select any text on a page, then right-click → Refine with Texter, or press Ctrl+Shift+R (Professional).

Voice dictation: Click into any text field, then right-click → Dictate here, or press Alt+Shift+D to start/stop. Set the dictation language above if it's not matching your browser's default.
